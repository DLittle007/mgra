Sustainable, High-Yield Mineral Extraction in the Caribbean

M.G.R.A. operates an expansive 850-acre limestone reserve in Westmoreland Parish, Jamaica, supplying commercial, industrial, and government clients across the Americas. Family-owned and operated, the company provides a secure, consistent, and quality-controlled material supply backed by modern processing infrastructure and deep cross-industry operational leadership.

Core Products

Boulder / Rip Rap

Heavy armor rock engineered for shoreline protection, breakwaters, erosion control, and large-scale civil works.

Crushed Stone (-3/4")

High-durability angular aggregate graded for asphalt mixes, structural concrete, and road base construction.

Gravel / Chips (3/16" to 3/8")

Precision-screened aggregate chips for specialized precast, asphalt surface courses, and high-performance drainage applications.

Manufactured Concrete Sand (ASTM C-33 | -3/16" + #200)

Clean, high-performance fine aggregate strictly manufactured to meet ASTM C33 specifications for structural concrete and civil applications.

Block Sand (-3/8")

Uniformly graded aggregate sand tailored for concrete block manufacturing and masonry structural units.

Rendering Sand / Fine Powder (-#14 / 1.5mm)

Ultra-fine, consistent material optimized for plastering, interior/exterior rendering, and specialized industrial mixes.

Capabilities

Processing Capacity & Plant

Powered by an advanced, dry-process aggregate plant with a production capacity of up to 400 TPH (tons per hour), driven by high-efficiency hybrid Cummins diesel/electric genset systems.

Equipment & Fleet

  • Primary Crushing: Taurian Terra Track 300J Mobile Jaw Crusher (32" x 44")
  • Secondary/Tertiary Impact: Terra Track i-130 RS 5048 Impactor on Ti-1110W / 250 kW Wheeled Chassis
  • Classification & Sizing: Terra Track 1662 3D Vibrating Screen (5' x 20' triple deck) and 2D HYPER Frequency Fine Screening Unit (6' x 18' double deck)

Quality Control

Multi-stage dry vibrating and high-frequency classification ensuring strict particle size distribution, zero-moisture dry-process consistency, and continuous gradation monitoring.

Leadership Experience

Leadership collectively brings 45–50 years of experience in heavy industry, spanning equipment operation and maintenance, process transformation, and operations management.

Quality & Testing

Chemical composition is verified per ASTM C25 chemical assay analysis; physical, sieve, and gradation testing is certified to ASTM C33 standards to meet stringent domestic and international export specifications.
