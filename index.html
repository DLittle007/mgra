Project Overview

The Mount Cromwell Reserve Extraction Project is more than a limestone quarry. It is built as a long-term industrial minerals and construction materials platform with an estimated 714 years of proven reserves.

The Problem

Many leading Jamaican limestone quarries are owned by multinational operators that do not consistently reinvest resources into local industrial growth.

The Solution

M.G.R.A. is focused on vertically integrated, Jamaica-manufactured outputs for domestic infrastructure and export markets, aligned with Vision 2030 and major island projects.

Core Products

Boulder / Rip Rap

For erosion control, sea-wall retention, and protected access on infrastructure projects.

Crushed Stone

Stable base layer beneath asphalt and concrete roads, corridors, and highways.

Gravel / Chips

Used by municipalities for sidewalks, parking lots, utility lines, and underground pipe routes.

Fine Sand

Fine aggregate for concrete, mortar, and masonry production workflows.

Ground Powder

Inputs for paper, paints, plastics, rubber, ceramics, and pharmaceutical manufacturing.
